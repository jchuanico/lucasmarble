Flag Conquest

Pick your nation. Roll the dice. Rule the world.

How to play

  • Each turn you get new troops. Tap your lands to place them.
  • Attack a neighbour from a land with 2 or more troops. You each roll one die.
  • Higher roll wins and the loser drops a troop. The same roll is a draw, so roll again.
  • Clear every troop from a land to plant your flag there.
  • Hold a whole continent for bonus troops. Defeat all three rival empires to win.

How to play

  • Place your new troops by tapping your own lands.
  • Tap one of your lands with 2 or more troops, then tap a glowing neighbour to attack.
  • You each roll one die. Higher roll wins and the loser drops a troop. A draw means roll again.
  • Take a land and your army marches in, leaving 1 troop behind.
  • Continent bonuses: North America +2, South America +2, Europe +5, Africa +3, Asia +6, Oceania +1.
  • Drag to move the map, pinch or scroll to zoom, and use the arrows to turn it.
