Flag Conquest

Sixty-four nations.
One marble survives.

A knockout tournament of flag marbles racing down a gauntlet of pegs, ramps, spinners and funnels. Pick your country, then watch the field get cut in half, heat by heat, until one champion rolls across the line.

Format15 heats of 8 marbles. Top 4 advance from each heat until the Grand Final.
PhysicsReal 16 mm glass marbles on a 4 m course under 9.81 m/s² gravity, with measured bounce, rolling and friction. No two races run the same.
Photo finishesMarbles within 100 ms of each other count as a tie, and a tie is settled by country stats.
HazardsA hydraulic crusher, a saw blade and a trapdoor sit on the course. Roughly one marble in twenty is destroyed before the line and is out of the heat.
Tie-breakersPopulation → land area → highest point → shared borders → lowest crime index → coin flip.
